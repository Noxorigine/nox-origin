## Research Question

Can high-resolution micro-X-ray fluorescence (µ-XRF) elemental profiling (specifically Mg/Ca, Sr/Ca, and detrital indicators like Ti/Ca) combined with stable isotope micro-sampling reliably distinguish anthropogenic aqueduct cleaning hiatuses from drought-induced growth cessations?

## Why

Paleoclimate reconstructions derived from aqueduct sinters risk misinterpreting human intervention—such as periodic mechanical descaling, channel repair, or artificial water diversions—as natural climatic droughts. Resolving this distinction is essential to prevent false signals in Mediterranean paleoclimate models and to accurately reconstruct Roman municipal maintenance schedules.

## Interdisciplinary Connection

Materials Science and Civil Infrastructure Engineering (industrial scale formation and pipe fouling dynamics). The physical mechanisms of calcium carbonate precipitation in ancient masonry channels mirror modern industrial scaling inside pipelines, where fluid velocity, chemical additives, and mechanical scrubbing alter deposit micro-textures and bonding interfaces.
